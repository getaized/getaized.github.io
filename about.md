# About GetAIzed

GetAIzed is an approachable, warm-hearted blog about artificial intelligence. We focus on bridging the gap between high-level research and everyday applications, sharing easy-to-understand guides, reviews of interesting tools, and stories about how AI is changing our world. We believe that technology should be accessible to everyone, and we're here to help you get inspired and feel at home with AI!
